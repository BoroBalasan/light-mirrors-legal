Privacy Policy — Light & Mirrors

Effective date: 2026-10-01 (version 1.0.2). Applies to the Light & Mirrors mobile game ("the App") published by Boris Balasanyan ("the Developer"). The previous version (effective 2026-09-17) described an App without analytics; this version adds the "Analytics and crash reporting" section.

Summary

The App has no user accounts, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and the Developer runs no servers. Everything the App saves (your solved levels, stars, daily challenge results, your chosen name and avatar, and your settings) stays on your device. The App uses two Google services, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, to count how the game is played and to receive crash reports; they receive technical data described below, never your name or e-mail address.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress: which levels you have solved, star ratings, daily challenge results and achievements.
  • Settings: theme, language, sound, music and vibration preferences.
  • Guest profile: the display name and avatar you pick in the App. They are stored only on the device and are never uploaded.

These files never leave your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them. The App also offers a "Reset progress" option in Settings that erases the progress file.

Analytics and crash reporting

This section applies from version 1.0.2 of the App onwards. Earlier versions collected nothing and sent no data anywhere.

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, both provided by Google, to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. Firebase Analytics receives app interaction events such as which levels you start, finish or leave, how long a level takes, how many moves and hints it took, tutorial steps, booster use and daily-challenge results. Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report when the App crashes: the technical stack trace, device model, operating system version, and a small set of in-app values such as your highest completed level, attached to help reproduce the problem.

Both services receive a Firebase installation identifier, a random identifier tied to this installation of the App rather than to you, and on Android Firebase Analytics also reads the device's advertising identifier, which you can reset or delete in your device's Google settings. Google processes this data on the Developer's behalf; the Developer sees it only as aggregate statistics and crash listings in the Firebase console and cannot identify an individual player from it. By Firebase's default retention settings, event-level data is kept for 2 months and identifier-linked data for 14 months, after which Google deletes it automatically. Details: Firebase data privacy and security and Google Privacy Policy.

Data the App does not collect

The App does not collect your name, e-mail address, precise location, contacts, photos or files, and does not read your messages. It shows no advertising, uses no cookies, and sends no data to the Developer's own systems, because the Developer has none. The only data that leaves your device is the analytics and crash data described above.

Network access

The App uses the Internet permission to send the analytics and crash data described above to Google, and so that links inside the App (this privacy policy) can open in your browser. It makes no other network requests.

Feedback by e-mail

Settings contains a "Send feedback" link. It opens your own mail application with the Developer's address pre-filled. Anything you send is a normal e-mail that you compose and control; the Developer uses it only to answer you and keeps it only as long as needed for that purpose.

Leaderboard

The daily leaderboard shown in this version contains simulated players generated on your device. No score or name is uploaded to a leaderboard; your daily-challenge result reaches Google only as an anonymous analytics event as described above.

Google Play

The App is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data in connection with the download and installation according to its own policy: Google Privacy Policy. The Developer does not receive personal data from this process.

Children

The App is a logic puzzle suitable for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, contains no features that require age verification, and collects no personal information from anyone; the analytics data described above is not linked to a person.

Future features

Later versions may add optional sign-in with Google Play Games (to keep your progress in your account and to rank on a world leaderboard) and advertising. If that happens, this policy will be updated before the feature is released, the App will ask for any consent required by law, and the Google Play data safety section will be updated to match.

Your rights

The Developer holds no personal data about you. If you want the analytics data associated with your installation deleted before Google's automatic retention limit, or have any question about privacy, contact the Developer at borisbalasanyankw@gmail.com; the Developer can request deletion from Google through the Firebase console. You can also reset your advertising identifier in your device settings, and uninstalling the App stops all collection.
